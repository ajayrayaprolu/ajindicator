now I understand the actual requirement, and I think the previous idea of simply “consolidating thresholds” needs one important correction.

Your problem is not really that you have too many threshold files. The real problem is that the application currently doesn't give you a clear answer to:

“Why did this trade pass or fail, which calculation mode produced the score, which conditions contributed, and which threshold blocked it?”

And without that visibility, you end up doing exactly what you described: Score mode + DEV/reduced thresholds, because AI/AI+SMC becomes so restrictive that you cannot tell whether the engine is intelligent or simply over-gated.

Keep the 3 calculation modes, but make them fundamentally different in how they calculate confidence, not three independent collections of gates.

2. AI mode should NOT mean “Score + 20 more mandatory conditions”

This is the biggest change I recommend.

AI mode should be:

        ↓

Strong momentum
Strong volume
Good trend
Good market sync
Normal spread

→ TRADE.

AI is therefore making the decision more selective, not impossible.

Keep all 3 modes. Keep Advanced Crypto. Keep Safe/Swing/Scalper. Keep OB/FVG/BOS/CHOCH/liquidity/sync.
