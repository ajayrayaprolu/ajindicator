One additional refinement

I would add one principle that governs the entire system:

Every engine should contribute evidence independently. No engine should directly override another. The Confidence Engine aggregates evidence, the Risk Qualification Engine vetoes unsafe conditions, and the Trade Authority makes the final execution decision.

That separation of responsibilities will make the system easier to maintain, easier to test, and much more adaptable as you add new brokers, indicators, or market concepts in the future. It also ensures that visual features and educational explanations remain decoupled from the core decision logic, so the underlying trading engine stays clean and extensible.

Exactly.

Trading modes are NOT removed

Your architecture still supports

Confidence Mode
AI Mode
AI + SMC Mode

Only the implementation changed.

Earlier

Builder
   ↓
Score calculations

Now

Builder
   ↓
Raw evidence

ConfidenceEngine
   ↓
confidence

AIConfidenceEngine
   ↓
AI decision

ExecutionAuthority
   ↓
approval

ExecutionEngine
   ↓
execution

That is a huge difference.

So the AI fields are not legacy because AI disappeared.

They are legacy because they are produced in the wrong place.

Therefore I would NOT delete AI fields yet.

Instead we need to determine

Who owns them now?
